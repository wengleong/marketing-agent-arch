D&A Operating Philosophy — "Vibe Accounting"

D&A staff are accountants who have taken on a workflow engineering role. Their accounting background gives them the intuition to recognise when something looks wrong — a balance that doesn't make sense, a GST figure that's oddly high. But their job is building and running the system that produces the work, not doing the accounting themselves.

✅ The Job

  • → Build and improve agent workflows
  • → Monitor outputs and catch anomalies
  • → Fix the agent when it gets something wrong
  • → Escalate genuine edge cases to A&A
  • → Own client comms for Tier 1

✗ Not the Job

  • ✗ Manually preparing journal entries
  • ✗ Coding transactions by hand
  • ✗ Patching one-off errors without fixing the agent
  • ✗ Making technical accounting judgments
  • ✗ Being the last line of quality defence
🔧

When D&A hits an error or discrepancy

Could this recur?

→ Fix the agent. Document it. Never patch manually.

Genuine one-off edge case?

→ Escalate to A&A. Don't waste a workflow fix on something the agent will never see again.

Not sure what's wrong?

→ Escalate to A&A. D&A "vibes" the output — A&A makes the call.

⚠ Note: Early on, D&A will need to do some manual work to understand what the agent should be producing. This front-loads the accounting knowledge requirement — it fades as workflows mature and the agent accumulates edge-case handling.

🎯

A&A Operating Philosophy — "AI Prepares, I Conclude"

A&A staff use AI heavily — but differently from D&A. Where D&A uses AI to produce work, A&A uses AI to prepare for their judgment. Every hour spent on research, assembly, or first drafts is an hour taken from advising clients and making professional calls. AI handles the preparation; A&A owns the conclusion.

📋 QC Review

AI pre-flags anomalies and variance movements. A&A reviews the flags — not every line. Judgment on what matters; AI handles what's mechanical.

📚 Technical Research

AI researches FRS standards, IRAS rulings, ACRA requirements and drafts the brief. A&A reads, concludes, and signs off the treatment. Never starts from a blank page.

🤝 Client Advisory

AI prepares meeting briefs, drafts advisory letters, monitors regulatory updates. A&A walks in informed, personalises the advice, and delivers it.

What A&A should never do manually if AI can prepare it first

Research FRS standards from scratch Manually compile QC review files Write first-draft advisory letters Manually compile client history pre-meeting Assemble filing packages by hand Draft routine client correspondence
2–3 : 1

D&A to A&A ratio — middle of the three practices

More judgment-embedded than CorpSec, but no hard regulatory floor like Compliance. Ratio shifts by service line — bookkeeping and payroll sit closer to 3:1; FS/MA and tax sit closer to 2:1. Blended across a typical portfolio: ~2–2.5:1.

Key Risks to Manage
⚠️
Knowledge gap at escalation
D&A builders need enough accounting literacy to know what to escalate. Deliberate upskilling + a clear escalation decision tree from A&A. Don't assume it'll be obvious to them.
⚠️
Piloting the wrong service line first
Start with bookkeeping — not FS, not tax advisory. High volume, most standardisable, errors are correctable. Add complexity only after the model is proven.
⚡
D&A builders getting stuck in pure tech
They're accountants who build, not developers. Ensure a tech/ops support person is available for the integrations work. Builders focus on the workflow logic, not the plumbing.
⚡
A&A becomes the scaling bottleneck
As D&A volume scales, A&A capacity could cap throughput. Design QC to be risk-based (not 100% review of every line) and track QC time per job. Plan A&A headcount accordingly.
💡
Team identity and morale
Avoid "factory" framing. Both practices are professional. Emphasise self-selection — people go where their personality fits, not where they're placed. Clear paths between roles exist.
Workflow IP
The most valuable asset the D&A team creates. This is the firm's proprietary execution engine — not just documentation.
🤖
Agent Workflow Library
Each service line documented as a versioned workflow: inputs, agent steps, decision points, exception triggers, outputs. Owned by D&A. Reviewed after every major QC finding. Version-controlled.
📐
Prompt & Template Bank
Tested, versioned prompts for every AI-assisted task. FS and MA templates locked by A&A, executed by D&A. Tax and GST computation templates with standard treatment rules baked in.
🚦
Escalation Decision Trees
Jointly owned: A&A defines what constitutes an escalation, D&A uses it in the field. Covers bookkeeping, GST, payroll, and tax. Updated quarterly or after any novel escalation case.
✅
QC Checklists
A&A-owned. Service-line specific. Used by A&A for final review but also shared with D&A so they know what's being checked — reduces surprises and educates builders on quality standards.
📖
Onboarding Playbook
New D&A hire should be productive within 2 weeks using this alone. Covers: how the workflows run, common exceptions, who to escalate to, tools and access, client communication guidelines.
📊
Workflow Performance Log
Running record of QC findings categorised by type (workflow fix / template update / one-off). Tracks error rates over time per service line. The data that proves the model is improving — and where it needs attention.

Ownership Model

D&A creates and maintains the workflow IP. A&A defines the standards it's built to. Neither team owns it entirely — it's a shared asset, but D&A is the custodian. As the pilot matures, this IP base becomes the competitive moat: the firm's ability to onboard, scale, and replicate consistently.

Compliance
Regulatory accountability wall is hard — certain decisions legally cannot be delegated. D&A owns all preparation; A&A owns all decisions.
⚖️

Non-Delegable Decisions

Under MAS AML/CTF requirements, risk classification, EDD sign-off, and STR/SAR decisions must sit with qualified compliance personnel. This is regulatory accountability — not a quality preference. The A&A compliance team cannot be thinned below what's required to hold this obligation. Model accordingly.

D&A owns
Compliance Operations
Document-driven, deadline-managed, well-tooled
Agent-Led Preparation
  • CDD document collection, organisation, and completeness checks
  • Sanctions & PEP screening via API — agent runs, D&A reviews hits
  • CDD form population from collected documents
  • Adverse news monitoring — agent surfaces flags, D&A triages
  • Periodic review scheduling and document chasing
  • FATCA/CRS data collection, preliminary matching, form population
D&A Boundaries
  • Escalate all risk classification inputs to A&A — never decide independently
  • Flag sanctions/PEP hits immediately — no clearance authority
  • Prepare the file; A&A makes the call
A&A owns
Compliance Decisions
Named compliance function — legally accountable

  • STR/SAR decisions — exclusively, no AI involvement
  • MAS regulatory reporting and submissions
  • Annual compliance programme oversight
